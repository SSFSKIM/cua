# Gate #259: a coordinate click landed a text line low in the Electron fixture

- **Ticket:** [#259](https://github.com/SSFSKIM/cua/issues/259), "macOS engine: a coordinate click lands one text line
  (~16 px) low in the Electron fixture; the vendor's lands on target" (P1). Gate of the macOS default switch (M5
  pre-flight §6), task T8e.
- **State:** the witness cell is built into the native matrix (`d0942e93b`) and unit-proven. Its hosted reading waits on sitting C's
  pilot. No engine change. **Open.**

## The report and its last reading

On 2026-11-01, in #187's slot (cell 3 (ii)), the Electron fixture was frontmost: a 720 × 560 window at (140, 100),
scale 2. The click was `click([120, 168])`, which is window point (60, 84). The target was line 2, column 5, about
offset 25.

- The vendor's caret landed at 25, on target.
- cua's caret landed at 39: line 3, column 5, one line (~16 px) low. It went by route 3, synthesized at the point.

The 2026-11-05 re-slot ran the same click on cua, from the caret at the value's start, under ABC. It **landed on
target**, at line 2, column 5.

- The field's scroll was 0 pt with the caret at either end.
- `cua/coordinateClick` showed the placement consistent from the screenshot point to the posted event:
  - screenshot point (120, 168) px;
  - window point (60, 84) pt;
  - posted (200, 184), the window frame's origin plus the window point;
  - the window frame unchanged before the post.

The 2026-11-01 miss is unexplained: not the field's scroll, and not a frame change before the post. The ticket is
open.

## What the code says today

The conversion is the one #259's diagnostic recorded:

- `coordinatePlacement` (`native/engine/Sources/Engine/ElementInput.swift:295-306`) maps the screenshot point to the
  window-local point. The posted point is the window frame's origin plus that local point.
- The Decision Log entry of 2026-11-01 (#259's diagnostic) left the conversion unchanged.

Later coordinate-click work refuses a stale point or a covered one and posts nothing then (#296, #330). None of it moves
the mapping. Nothing in the code shows a line's offset. Whether the miss still occurs is the runner's question.

## The witness

**The cell:** `electron/frontmost/click.point` (`scripts/native-matrix/plan.mjs`, `CARET.point`), a method case of the
full matrix. It runs in the frontmost regime only, as the ticket registered it. In the background a web page takes no
pointer, and that is the declared refusal's question.

1. **Prepare.** The fixture's field is given the matrix's known value (`FIELD_LINES`, eight lines) and focused. The
   caret is put at the value's end (`selectText` of the last word, `cursor_after`). This is 2026-11-01's condition,
   where the miss was seen.
2. **The point.** The screenshot is read, and the window point (60, 84) is scaled into its pixels. A screenshot that is
   not the fixture window's shape is refused, and nothing is sent.
3. **Device read before the click.** The approved bundle's `--probe text-view` reads the field's AXSelectedTextRange,
   outside either engine (`matrix-device.mjs text-range`, `caret.mjs`). It must read the caret at the value's end, or
   the row is invalid and nothing is sent.
4. **Act.** One coordinate click, `__app.click([x, y])`.
5. **Device read after the click.**
6. **Check.** The value is read back and must be unchanged.

**What it reads.** The row's outcome is the reading: `range`, `collapsed`, the caret's `line` and `column` (0-based),
and `onTarget` (a caret on line 2). Also `valueKept`. The row also carries `caret {before, after}`. On cua the act's
engine meta keeps `cua/coordinateClick` (`engineMeta.act.coordinateClick`), so a difference carries its placement.
These are offsets, booleans and points only.

**How it is judged.**

- **The row's own check:** a caret on the aimed line, whatever its column.
- **The verdict** is the T10a judge's, in the mode added for the caret rows (`cell-verdict.mjs` `'reading'`; see
  [258.md](258.md)).
  - It works as any frontmost row against the vendor's sample of the same run, with the NO VERDICT rules unchanged.
  - Two passes are equal only with the same reading, so a caret one column off from the vendor's is worse.
  - The ticket's success criterion is "lands on the target's line and column, and matches the vendor".
- **No departure may name the row.**

**Where it runs.**

- In every full matrix run on both lanes: sitting C's pilot once a lane, the full sitting three times.
- If the vendor is not live on a runner, the same cell runs in the first MacBook lane:
  `node scripts/native-matrix.mjs cell method --scene electron …` on each lane.

The ticket also asked for TextEdit and Chrome for Testing cells. They are not built here: the gate's witness is the
Electron cell where the miss was seen. If the reading shows a difference, the fix's proof adds them.
