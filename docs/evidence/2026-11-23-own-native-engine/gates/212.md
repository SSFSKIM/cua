# Gate #212: a save sheet completing across an intervention

- **Ticket:** [#212](https://github.com/SSFSKIM/cua/issues/212), "Verify unintended save-sheet completion across native
  intervention and report partial effects" (P1), with PR [#218](https://github.com/SSFSKIM/cua/pull/218). Gate of the
  macOS default switch (M5 pre-flight §6), task T8c.
- **State:** PR #218 re-cut on `feat/117-m5` and unit-proven. The hosted cell is prepared and has not run. Until a run
  reads the Save panel's Go To controls, the cell only observes (below). **Open.**

## The report

From MAWS's dogfood (`l6-artifacts.md`, item 14), on cua's engine and host:

1. A native save sheet was open.
2. The agent called `setValue(saveAsNameTextField, <path>)`.
3. The call failed with `-10016 The user changed …`.
4. The sheet then closed, and a file with the default name was in Downloads. The reporter did not click Save.

The ticket's reading, which still holds: a plausible partial-action incident, not a proven engine-made Save. The
report has no per-call meta and no input trace, so who committed the sheet is not known. The engine's part was this:
the pre-action refusal and the stop between steps both answered with the same sentence. A null action interval was
the only hint, and it does not cover preparation (the AXFocused write, the focus fallback's click, route 3's
activation and its click). The answer could not say whether anything had happened.

## The change

The vendor's sentence still opens every userIntervened answer. One clause follows it:

| Where it stopped | Clause |
|---|---|
| The pre-action check, no step reached | `The requested input was refused before its first step.` |
| The pre-action check, but the request ended a held activation | `The action was refused before its first step. What may have taken effect: the end of a held activation, which may have sent the app a deactivation. Nothing was undone.` |
| Between steps (the in-flight check) | `The action was stopped part-way. What may have taken effect: <steps>. Nothing was undone.` When no step is named: `Any step it had begun may have taken effect.` |

The refusal does not say "nothing was done". The execute prologue can re-enable a Chromium app's accessibility before
the method's guard runs (`Methods.swift` `executeRequest`, `Enablement.swift`), and that is no step of the input, so it
is not in the account. What the clause does claim is narrower: the requested input took none of its steps.

The steps (`InputEffect`, `native/engine/Sources/Engine/Intervention.swift`) are the ones the request reached:

- a scroll into view;
- route 3's activation, and its own clicks: at the window's activation point, or the inert one just outside the
  window (early, on a join, or as a hold unwinds);
- the field's focus (the AXFocused write, or a WebKit field's caret or press, #324);
- the focus fallback's click;
- set_value's value write;
- the action's own input;
- a held activation ended by the request. It "may have" sent a deactivation: the release counter moves before the
  release, and a remaining holder keeps the activation.

Route 3's activation and its clicks are read from what was actually posted, not from the meta's records.
`ActivationPosts` (`Activation.swift`) counts the fake activation's subtype-1 posts and its clicks on the request's
thread. `InputEffects` reads the change since the meta began. `execute` reads it again for the whole request, which
covers a join that consumes a pointer holder's arm and a hold that unwinds with its click still armed. A joiner that
shares a consumed activation posted nothing and is named with nothing.

The input meta carries the same account as `interruption: {phase: "beforeAction" | "inFlight", effects: [...]}`. The
service forwards it as `cua/interruption`, a snapshot of the interrupted action: the account plus that action's
`inputRoute`, `actionInterval`, `receivedAt`, `hidIdleMs` and activation data (`activationGuardMs`,
`activationClick`, `activationClickKind`, `activationJoin`). It is the one key not reset per call, so it stays standing
through the rest of the execution, including the observation the answer advises in the same cell. The host starts the
response meta fresh for each execution (`privileged-node-repl.mjs`, `execState.responseMeta`), so nothing carries into
the next one. Every other `cua/*` key stays per call. The secret wrapper withholds the engine's text, so it repeats the
account in the same fixed phrases. For a refusal with no step it says "it was refused before its first step; nothing was
entered". It never says "nothing was entered" next to a named step. The secret counts as possibly entered once its
write, or the action's own input, may have gone.

## Hosted cell (prepared, not run)

The cell is `intervention-save-sheet` (`scripts/native-matrix/probe-plan.mjs`, `probe-run.mjs` `saveSheetAttempt`). It
is its own registered cell, not a row of the full matrix. Its row is `textedit/intervention.saveSheet`, in the
`intervention` section. TextEdit is in front, and the cell runs these steps:

1. super+n opens an untitled document, the sheet variant's typing cell types into it, and super+s opens the Save sheet.
   Each of these goes under the key guard.
2. The name field's index is kept from that observation (`ID: saveAsNameTextField`; the save sheet is the panel without
   `DontSaveButton`).
3. The sheet's destination is moved to a folder the attempt makes (`cua-212-save-*` under the runner's real temporary
   directory), through the panel's Go To dialog. Each control is identified positively, by its identifier and role
   word and its place in the tree (`SAVE_PANEL_CONTROLS`, `savePanelReading` in `probe-plan.mjs`). The observation's
   grammar prints no raw role or subrole, so the role word stands in for them. The controls are the panel around the
   name field, the Go To dialog inside the panel, the dialog's path field inside the dialog, and the panel's location
   pop-up. After super+shift+g, the dialog and its path field must be identified and the field must be the focused
   element before anything is typed. After typing, on the observation just before the Return, the field must still be
   focused, hold the path exactly, and the name field must read as before. Afterwards the dialog must be gone and the
   panel's location pop-up, not a listing entry, must name the folder. Anything else fails closed: no Return, the row is
   invalid, and nothing is sent toward the sheet.

   **Observation only, for now.** The controls have not been read on a runner, so `SAVE_PANEL_CONTROLS` holds none. With
   none, the cell opens the Go To dialog and records the panel's structure before and after (`outcome.observed`: per
   element line its index, depth, parent, `ID:` part, role word and bare title, and whether it is focused; never a
   value). It types nothing, sends no Return, and the row is invalid. The pilot's first run is therefore
   observation-only. The controls are read from its `outcome.observed`, and only then can the cell act.
4. At the cell's time: `setValue(<name field>, "cua-212-intended")`. The probe's keystroke (`qz`, no Return) is aimed into
   TextEdit's front window, 10 ms after the call on the first attempt. Later attempts aim it 5 ms after the receipt they
   measured.
5. After a 2 s settle, the sheet is read again, and so is the run's folder.
6. The row is recorded. Then, whatever happened: the debounce is waited out, TextEdit is observed, Escape cancels the
   sheet (again for a Go To dialog left up), the run's folder is removed when it is empty, and the untitled document is
   closed with its Delete button. Nothing is meant to be saved, and a save that did commit lands in the run's folder.

The judgement:

- **Valid on cua's lane** only when the probe's measured post came after the engine received the request
  (`cua/receivedAt`) and before the cell had its response (`endAt`). A stop before the write reports no action interval,
  so none is required. The row keeps `postedAt`, `receivedAt`, `callAt`, `endAt` and the offsets between them.
- **cua passes** when the answer is the user-changed sentence with "The action was stopped part-way." and "may have
  taken effect", the structured account agrees (`cua/interruption` phase `inFlight`), the sheet is still open after
  the settle, and the run's folder is empty. The row also keeps the answer, `cua/interruption` and whether the name
  was written.
- **A sentence and an account that disagree** fail the row: one says stopped part-way and the other does not.
- **Invalid, and retried:** a post outside that window, a refusal before the action, or a set_value that returned
  before the probe's event was seen. Three such attempts leave the row NO VERDICT.
- **A sheet gone, or a file in the run's folder,** fails the row whatever the answer or the timing said. It is never
  retried, and the row keeps why the timing alone would not have judged it (`outcome.validity`).
- **A cleanup that throws** (a save that renamed the document, so the untitled one cannot be closed) comes after the
  row is recorded. Its failure goes on the row (`outcome.cleanupFailed`) and stops the run, so it is also among the
  run's problems. The same holds for a preparation that failed before the act.
- **The vendor's lane** never stops an action in flight. Its row records its answer and the sheet, and it is valid when
  the post fell inside the call.
